STEP 4 IS TRIPPING ME UP ... WHY DO WE NEED IT.. I UNDERSTAND ITS A WEBSOCKET KIND OF RELAY FOR THE SSH TCP CONNECTION?

Y, exactly — that's correct.

Step 2's cloudflared tunnel on the tablet wraps your raw TCP (SSH) traffic into Cloudflare's protocol so it can ride over HTTPS/WebSocket OUT to their edge
Step 4's cloudflared access tcp on the PC is the reverse adapter — it unwraps that same protocol back into plain TCP, and exposes it as a normal local port (e.g. localhost:8022 on the PC side)

So: step 4 is the "translator" sitting between Cloudflare's relay and your normal SSH client

Both ends need a cloudflared process: one to wrap (tablet), one to unwrap (PC). That's why it's not optional.

Likely just lighter load characteristics, not a different network path:

Same underlying broken IPv6 path likely affects both, but SSH is far more sensitive to any hiccup since it's continuous and interactive, while HTTP requests are short bursts that can complete in the IPv4-fallback window before you'd ever notice
